## B6 Off-screen panes and the minimap

### Off-screen panes and the minimap guidelines

- **Kansō** Edges show which panes are past them: chevron, then tiles 20 with the program icon. Click a tile to go there, hover for its name. The state dots on the tiles go: the sidebar rows and the status bar already say who waits.
- **Kansō** The hover card goes ("2 panes beyond the right edge" over a list of the same tiles). It was a hover card, which spec §7 rules out, and its title counted rows already in view.
- **Kokō** A pane dragged over an edge washes it in accent 25%, as every drop zone does. Piece strip.edges, S/H, default S.
- **Yūgen** Minimap, piece strip.minimap S/H, default S: it is a control (a pill jumps to its column, the stepper sets panes per view). Focused column fg, in view muted, out of view input, stacks as 2 px cells.
- **Kansō** The "− 2 +" stepper stays on the bar. Its tooltips lose the teaching ("Fewer panes per view" becomes the key, ⌘⇧−). The right-click menu adds the same choice as a segmented control, and Even out.
- **Seijaku** "Strip paused" becomes the state pattern's LINE: mark, "Layout paused", a small Resume. It still resumes alone after 10 s.
- **Kokō** Remote: 0. Layout only.
