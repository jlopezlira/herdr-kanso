### Ports guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Kansō** The list is the panel. No pane first, said once as that section's title; the rows in panes follow after a gap, each named by its pane.
- **Kansō** No hero. "4 servers", "1 with no pane", "held now", "opened today" counted the rows under them.
- **Kansō** A row is one line: port, what runs it (a word: storybook, next; or the pane's name) and its size. On pointer or selection the second line shows the command, folder and age, and the actions: No pane, Stop is the one primary; in a pane, three quiet links, Go to first.
- **Kansō** Stop and Stop 2 arm "Press again" in accent, 4 s. A stop that did not take says so in blocked and offers Force stop.
- **Seijaku** The status bar's:6006 no pane opens here with that row selected and Stop focused.
- **Yūgen** Chart, history and the share story sit behind History, closed; its range is the kit segmented control, like every period switch. Its summary shows when it opens.
- **Kansō** Share is a menu from the header, worded "Counts and sizes only". The note under the buttons goes.
- **Seijaku** The focused project's computer decides what is read: devbox's ports when it lives there, named beside the title. Unreachable keeps the last read at 55% with Stop disabled.
- **Shizen** Narrow under 340: title 15, the command hides, "Open in the browser" becomes "Open".
- **Kokō** Cost: open and in front, one lsof + ps every 30 s. Behind or closed: none. Another computer: one remote listener read (about 1 KB) every 60 s, ps only when the set of ports changed. History is one file read when the fold opens.

### At rest, measured

### Element · before → now · when it appears

- Share, Refresh: in the header → gone. pointer in the panel or keyboard focus, in the header.
- Scope "This computer": a line → gone. when the panel reads another computer: its name beside the title.
- No pane: the working dot: dot, coloured → gone. the title says it; colour stays for what needs you.
- Stop 2: beside No pane → gone. pointer on the No pane title.
- Command, folder, age: a second line per row → gone. pointer or selection on the row (its second line).
- What runs it: command in mono → one word: storybook, next. full command on pointer.
- In a pane: title: a section title → gone. the rows after the gap name their pane: checkout, web.
- Program icon (Claude, terminal): per row → gone. pointer on the row.
- History summary: 6 at the peak · 1.3 GB given back → History. History opened: its first line.
