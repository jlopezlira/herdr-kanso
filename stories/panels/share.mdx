### Share guidelines

- **Seijaku** One menu (the kit's, 252 wide) from the header's share icon, in Ports, Memory, Spend and the hjkl dōjō. Its header is the privacy fact: "Counts and sizes only" (Ports, Memory), "Totals and days only" (Spend), "Scores and practice days only" (dōjō, shown once a round is played; draws only the header icon).
- **Yūgen** The icon shows only when there is something to share: history in Ports and Memory, an unfiltered home page in Spend.
- **Seijaku** While the image is made, that row says so and the others dim; the result is the toast.
- **Kansō** The inline row of four bordered buttons and its sentence go.
- **Kokō** Cost: none until chosen; then a local render. The post opens in the browser.
