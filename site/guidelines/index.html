UX/UI guidelines

The elements kansō is drawn with. Reuse them instead of drawing new ones.

Only approved elements appear here. An element built in a version, or verified against the running app, says so.

  • Philosophykansō is named after one of the seven principles of Zen art described by Shin'ichi Hisamatsu in Zen and the Fine Arts (1971). The design follows all seven as one philosophy, not taste.
  • TokensThe only values kansō may use. Every size, space, radius, height, icon, switch, motion and colour comes from this set.
  • StorybookThe elements and their states, in dark and light.
