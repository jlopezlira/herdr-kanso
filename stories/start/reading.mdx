# How to read this Storybook

<PageStatus page="Start/How to read this Storybook" />

The sidebar follows how a screen is built: Start, Tokens, Primitives, Compositions, Panels, Layouts.

- **Overview** shows a page's states and their specifications; **Variants** with the **State** control shows one state at a time. The theme toolbar switches dark and light.
- Hover, focus, active, disabled, dimmed, loading, empty, failure and armed are states on their component's page, not pages of their own.
- Each page has one status, at its top and as a tag in the sidebar filter: **Ready** (approved and drawn in the kit), **In review** (drawn in the kit, waiting for approval), **Proposal** (a new design made here, not approved) and **Old look** (the app's current markup, not redesigned yet).
- **Proposed** marks a state with no approved drawing yet.
- Features that exist in the app but await a design are listed as **Not designed yet** under the page's prompt, with their code location. The full list is `stories/GAPS.md`.

Token sources: `kanso/tokens.json` (names, values, both themes), `kanso/mods/kanso-panel/ui.js` (`G`, `H`, `R`, `T`, `ICON`, `SWITCH`, `ARM_MS`), `kanso/themes/kanso.json` and `kanso/themes/kanso-day.json`.

## Prompt for agents

```text
Find the component by its role in the six sections. Read its Overview, check its states in both themes, then copy its prompt. Compose larger surfaces from the existing tokens and primitives.
```
