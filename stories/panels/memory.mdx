### Memory guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Yūgen** The free number is the panel's one big fact, with the total beside it; swap on pointer. It turns blocked under the guard's floor, and only then a primary appears: Recycle 2 idle · 1.7 GB back.
- **Kansō** The stats row goes. Herdr is one row with its total; Window and Core on pointer, or as rows when one is past its limit. Hung helpers keep their row with End. Recycles, restarts and freed move to History.
- **Yūgen** Agents follow after a gap, one line each: name and size. Over the guard's limit the size turns working; an agent that needs you keeps the blocked mark. Program, state and uptime show on pointer or selection; working lives in the sidebar.
- **Kansō** When the only idle agent is the one you are on, the section has no bulk link; its row says "you are on it" and keeps its own Recycle. The sentence "Only the agent you are on is idle…" goes.
- **Kansō** Recycle and Close show on the selected row; both arm "Press again" in accent. The sparks and the third bulk action ("Only the 2 over 500 MB") go: the guard does that on its own.
- **Yūgen** A batch draws in place: the spinning ensō while it runs, each agent's before and after, refusals in blocked, then Dismiss.
- **Shizen** Guard folds to one line, Guard on (with any switch that is off: "restart off"); opened, its three switches with their limits in the label, writing the guard's on.
- **Seijaku** Off: one line and Turn on. Off, unreadable and stopped share it; a failed install prints the installer's words.
- **Shizen** History folds the chart (lowest bars in blocked, the lowest marked in accent) and Events, one 32 row each with its time; refusals in blocked, the low notice in working.
- **Kansō** Heaviest processes go: no action follows from them in kansō. The low notice already names the heaviest.
- **Kansō** No guard on this platform (Linux): the panel is absent, not a sentence.
- **Kokō** Cost: three small local files every 15 s while open and in front. Another computer with the guard installed: one on read there every 60 s (about 3 KB); history only when the fold opens.

### At rest, measured

### Element · before → now · when it appears

- Share, Refresh: in the header → gone. pointer in the panel or keyboard focus.
- Scope "This computer": a line → gone. another computer: its name beside the title.
- Swap: beside the number → gone. pointer on the number.
- Herdr: title, Window, Core: four lines → one row: Herdr 1.4 GB. pointer on the row: Window 1.1 GB, Core 210 MB.
- 2 hung helpers · 60 MB · End: a row → unchanged. it needs you: stays.
- Agents title, Recycle 2 idle: a section title → gone (a gap). pointer on the agents: Recycle 2 idle beside the first.
- Program icon per agent: Claude, Codex → gone. pointer on the row (its second line).
- State and uptime per agent: a second line → gone. pointer or selection on the row; working lives in the sidebar.
- cart-ui needs you: mark and words → the blocked mark. pointer on the row: "left as it is".
- docs 980 MB in working: coloured → unchanged. past the guard's 900 MB: stays.
- Guard title and three switches: four lines → Guard on. Guard on opened.
- History summary: lowest 18% · 6 recycles · 2.3 GB freed → History. History opened.
