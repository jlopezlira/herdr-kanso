## Tab bar, the tabs

### Tab bar guidelines

- **Yūgen** Tabs carry no program icon and no working mark: the pane headers carry both, and the icon told no tab apart. A mark shows only when it asks or changed: Needs you, and Finished until seen. Piece tabs.marks, S.
- **Kansō** Close stays where it is: × on the active tab, at rest. On a tab that holds an agent the first press arms it for 4 s: the tab turns accent in its own box and the tooltip says "Press again to close checkout". Nothing changes width. A plain shell closes on one press.
- **Kansō** Double-click renames in place with the built-in Input, name selected. Enter keeps it, Esc puts it back. The dialog with its hint goes.
- **Kokō** Drag: the held tab stays on the pressed wash (w12) and a 2 px accent bar shows where it lands.
- **Shizen** A rename on another computer is drawn at once: the new name at 80% with a breathing dot until the core confirms. Refused, the old name comes back and the toast says so.
- **Kansō** The " Z" after a zoomed tab's name goes. The zoomed pane fills the tab and its header holds Unzoom.
- **Shizen** Tabs that do not fit fold behind +5, which opens them as a menu with their marks. + and Arrange sit outside the scroller.
- **Shizen** devbox connecting: one breathing skeleton per known tab. Unreachable: tabs at 55% (stale), and no +, because it cannot start anything there.
- **Kokō** Remote: 0 calls at rest. Tabs come over the core's own link.
- **Seijaku** Measured, unchanged: bar 48 (layouts/kanso.json:62), px 12, no fill of its own (tabs/main.js:406). A tab is 26 tall, 8 inside, 8 between icon, name and mark, text 12.5, icon 15, mark 14, name up to 170; 2 between tabs, 8 to + and Arrange. Panel buttons 26 with 2 between (:386, :418); the last one now sits 12 from the edge.

## Tab bar, panel buttons

### Tab bar guidelines

- **Seijaku** One button for all: 26 square, icon 15, hover w7, open w12. The name is the tooltip, with its key. Nothing changes width, so no button moves when you point at one or open one.
- **Yūgen** A closed panel button paints nothing: no fill, no border, the frame shows through. Only hover and open paint.
- **Kansō** The ? and the gear leave the tab bar and its end closes up. Settings has one door: the footer's Settings row, whose menu holds kansō settings (⌃⇧,) and Herdr settings. The ? opened the same dialog on Keys, so it was a second door; ⌘⇧/ still opens Keys.
- **Kokō** Drag to reorder shows the held button on the pressed wash and the accent bar, as tabs do.
- **Yūgen** Panels is the place a hidden panel comes back from: a check means shown, the key is ⌃⇧N in bar order, hover shows the grip, and a row drags to reorder. Hiding unmounts the panel, so its reads stop.
- **Shizen** Fold order as the bar narrows: tab names to 120, then to 72, then extra tabs into +N. Panel buttons, + and Arrange never fold: they stay visible at any width.
- **Seijaku** Window buttons are icons in the same 26 button, not "−" and "□" in the font, right after the panel buttons.
- **Seijaku** Order: Files, Spend, PR, Shipped, Worktrees, Ports, Containers, Memory, Images, then Simulator when a simulator tool exists (tabs/main.js:57-68), until the person drags them (:72-77). The examples show the default.
- **Kokō** Remote: 0. A hidden panel mounts nothing.
