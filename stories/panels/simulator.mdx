### Simulator guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Kansō** kansō's own, on the platform's tools only. iOS: the picture is xcrun simctl io screenshot; Open in Simulator appears on pointer over the device, for touch and typing. Android: the emulator's own picture, touch and drag on it, typing while it has focus (the kit's focus ring), and Back, Home, Rotate, volume and Lock under it. The serve-sim download through npx goes.
- **Seijaku** No toolbar on iOS: nothing there could act (simctl injects no touch).
- **Yūgen** The header: title and the device beside it (iPhone 16 Pro); Pin, Devices and Refresh appear with the pointer, the system on pointer over the device name. "Booted", Boot and Change stop sharing one 13.5 line.
- **Seijaku** Devices is a page with ‹: iOS and Android sections, On or Off per row, an unusable image dimmed with its reason.
- **Shizen** Off draws the device's outline with Boot in it. Booting breathes the same outline. A stalled picture stays at 55% with its time; it reconnects on its own and Refresh forces it.
- **Kansō** Behind other windows the picture stops being read (work, not motion) and resumes on return. "The stream stopped." and Reconnect go.
- **Seijaku** The device's corner follows the device (content, not chrome), so the frame's radius is not from the kit scale.
- **Kansō** The REMOTE and iOS-only captions go: another computer is named beside the title, and a computer with neither tool has no Simulator at all.
- **Kokō** Cost: local only, by nature: simulators run where Xcode and the Android SDK are. iOS: one screenshot a second while open and in front. Android: the emulator's frame stream while open and in front. Nothing remote.

### Element · before → now · when it appears

- Pin, Devices, Refresh: in the header → gone. pointer in the panel or keyboard focus.
- Scope: device, OS, computer: a line → iPhone 16 Pro beside the title. pointer: iOS 18.2; another computer: its name.
- Open in Simulator: a button → gone. pointer on the device.
