Use policy

kansō is made to help one person run many agents well. Use it in that spirit.

Last updated November 6, 2026

Please do

  • Use it for your own work and your team's, on machines and repositories you are allowed to work on.
  • Follow the terms of the AI tools and services you run in it, including their usage limits.
  • Share cards that show your real numbers.
  • Report a security problem privately first, through a GitHub security advisory, so it can be fixed before it is public.

Please don't

  • Run agents through it against systems, accounts or data you have no permission to touch.
  • Use it to get around a provider's limits, payments or safeguards.
  • Edit a share card to show numbers that aren't yours, or present a changed copy of kansō as the original.

Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome on GitHub. What you contribute is shared under the same Apache License 2.0. Be kind in issues and reviews: everyone here is making something for people who work with agents all day.
