### Containers guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Yūgen** One line says what Docker costs this computer: 2.1 GB held · 4.2 GB on disk; can grow and Quit Docker appear on pointer on that line, and the disk part opens Disk.
- **Kansō** No running, stopped and failed counts. Running rows carry no dot; a failed row keeps its blocked dot and reads exited 1 · 3 min in blocked, the status bar's words.
- **Kansō** A project is its title with "2 of 3 up" beside it; its compose file's Up, Rebuild, Down take the count's place on pointer. The project square goes: the name says it.
- **Shizen** Actions show on the selected row, by state. Failed: Logs is the primary (why it failed), then Start, Details, Remove. Running: Open (web ports), Logs, Shell, Restart, Stop. Details stay a fold inside the row.
- **Kansō** Down, Remove, Quit and the bulk removals arm "Press again" in accent. Quit says what it stops.
- **Yūgen** Images, volumes and build cache move behind Disk, opened from the held line. The pull field lives there, with Pull as its primary.
- **Kansō** A Docker context on another machine is named beside the title, once; its address on pointer. The yellow sentence goes; ports are shown, not copied.
- **Seijaku** Another computer (devbox) reads its docker there: a stated addition.
- **Kansō** No Docker installed: the panel is absent. The "Docker is not installed" line goes.
- **Kokō** Cost: open and in front, docker ps -a every 15 s; inspect only for new ids; stats every third read; images, volumes and system df only while Disk is open. Another computer: the same ps over the wire every 30 s (about 2 KB), nothing else at rest.

### At rest, measured

### Element · before → now · when it appears

- Refresh: in the header → gone. pointer in the panel or keyboard focus.
- Scope "This computer · Docker Desktop": a line → gone. another computer: its name beside the title; the engine in Disk.
- Held, can grow, Quit Docker: one line → 2.1 GB held · 4.2 GB on disk. can grow to 7.8 GB and Quit Docker on pointer on that line.
- Disk fold and its summary: a fold → gone. "4.2 GB on disk" opens it; 1.6 GB unused inside.
- Project letter square: per project → gone. the project name says it.
- compose.yaml row, Up, Rebuild, Down: a row → gone. pointer on the project title, in place of its count; "2 of 3 up" moves into the title.
- Running: green dot: per container → gone. running is normal; only a stopped or failed container keeps its blocked dot.
- Image, port, uptime: a second line → gone. pointer or selection on the row.
- api-db exited 1 · 3 min: blocked dot and words → unchanged. it needs you: stays loud.
