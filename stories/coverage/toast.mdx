## Prompt for agents

```text
Build the Notices surfaces for kansō from the kit in stories/proto.css and kanso/tokens.json.
Keep one component with its states as variants. Use the existing button, field, segmented, switch, Armed, row, state line/block, skeleton and exact sprite symbols.
At rest retain only what would be missed. Keep one fact in one home; colour only the thing asking for attention. Expose secondary actions on hover, selection or keyboard focus.
Use the global theme. Preserve keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior. Irreversible actions use the shared four-second Armed control; ordinary reversible actions take one press.
Failures retain source words beside their recovery action. Keep the last successful read during a refresh failure. An unavailable capability has no toolbar destination.
Do not copy the native renderer’s conflicting visual treatments. The states above are the reviewable kit composition; their source references identify the behavior they represent.
```
