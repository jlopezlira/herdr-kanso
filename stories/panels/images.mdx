### Images guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Kansō** The panel skeleton: title 20 with "1 of 3" beside it. Stay on this agent and Refresh appear with the pointer; the lock stays while you have pinned it. The Send button names the agent.
- **Kansō** The picture fills the width; a click drops a numbered pin, a drag a region, and the note is typed beside it: the one pin component of. The dialog overlay goes.
- **Kansō** On pointer over the picture: its name, folder and time, and Open, then a strip of 40 px thumbnails (← → too). The wordless coloured dots go.
- **Kansō** One primary that names the agent: Send to checkout, ⌘↩ beside it when it has focus. No pins, no button. Clear goes: Backspace on an empty note, or × on hover, removes a mark.
- **Seijaku** An agent that waits on you gets no Send; the line says "cart-ui needs you first", the PR panel's words.
- **Kokō** Another computer: nothing large crosses the wire unasked. The picture is a name, its size and Load; thumbnails are the picture's colour until loaded. Unreachable keeps the last picture at 55%.
- **Kansō** Empty states state facts: "No pictures in this conversation", "No agent in focus". The sentence that explained the panel goes.
- **Kokō** Cost: this computer, one stat of the conversation per change check, a read only when it changed. Another computer: one remote stat per check with backoff, the file list on change; a picture moves only on Load. Thumbnails are drawn from copies already made, never fetched for the strip.

### At rest, measured

### Element · before → now · when it appears

- Pin to this agent, Refresh: in the header → gone. pointer or focus; the lock stays while you have pinned it.
- Scope: agent, folder: a line → gone. the button names the agent; the folder on pointer on the picture.
- File: name, folder, time, Open: a row → gone. pointer on the picture, under it.
- Thumbnail strip: a row of three → 1 of 3 beside the title. pointer on the picture, or ← →.
- ⌘↩ on Send: a hint → gone. when Send has keyboard focus.
- Pins and notes: yours → unchanged. your own words: stay.
