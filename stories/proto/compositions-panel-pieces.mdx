## One panel skeleton

### Panel skeleton guidelines

- **Yūgen** Header 40: title 20 (15 under 340), at most two context icons, Refresh last; its tooltip says when it was read. A page inside a panel puts ‹ before its title.
- **Seijaku** Scope line 11.5 dim: the project, branch or computer, or what the page is. A stale read takes its place ("Read 14:02 · devbox unreachable · next try 14:07") and the body drops to 55%; writes are disabled.
- **Fukinsei** Period: the kit segmented control, natural width, with the stepper (two icon buttons) at its right; a past period adds Now at the number's right. Spend, Shipped and every History range use it; no text tabs. Under 340 the stepper moves to the number's line.
- **Kansō** Section: title 15, then at its right either one dim fact (a size, a date) or one quiet link (Clean up 1, Recycle 2 idle, Stop 2). Never a count of the rows below it.
- **Kansō** The one row: name 12.5 (truncates), the value at the right 12.5 tabular in foreground (a state colour when the value is a state), meta under the name in 11.5 dim. An optional mark goes first (a 6 dot, a program icon, an ensō, a project square) and the meta lines up under the name. Without meta it is the 32 one-line form. Rest; hover = 7% wash; selected = selected fill, and only then its actions line: at most one primary, the rest quiet, a removal arms "Press again" in accent. Spend, Worktrees, Memory, Shipped, Ports and Containers use it; built as prow2 plus the value class pbx-v.
- **Shizen** More: "Show 4 more" as a quiet link under the list. Fold: closed, its line carries the summary at the right.
- **Kokō** First read: the kit skeleton shaped like the rows it will hold. Empty: one muted line. Error: the tool's words in blocked, with Refresh.
- **Seijaku** Share: one menu from the header icon. A panel whose tool is missing does not exist.
- **Seijaku** Keys: j k gg G Enter Esc r. Enter runs the selected row's first action. No teaching footers.

## First screens: only what you would miss

### First screens guidelines

- **Kansō** Subtract information, never options. At rest a panel drops facts that repeat or say nothing now: scope lines, second lines, labels, marks and colour with no reason. Those facts come back under the pointer or keyboard. Every action and option, those the app has and those we added, stays visible and one press away. Colour stays only on what asks for you, and failures stay loud.
- **Seijaku** Measured in the browser on the six first screens: rows 103 → 76 · glyphs 35 → 19 · colours 33 → 25 · lines 150 → 88. Preview's 14 lines are the file itself, so its chrome is what halves: 2 rows to 1.
- **Kansō** One fact, one place. The folder, branch, files and lines are in the pane header, so the panels drop their scope and facts lines. Header icons, the period switch, Reply and Comment fields, Everyone's, Explain, Remove all and Prune all stay.
- **Kansō** Nothing is lost: each removed fact says where it lives now, and the options audit lists every control. Both are in panels-a-elements.md.

## Panels, subtracted

### Panels guidelines

- **Yūgen** At rest a panel shows what you would miss if it were gone. The rest appears when it matters: it needs you, it changed, or the pointer or keyboard is on it.
- **Yūgen** Header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus. A panel's one qualifier (the month, the device, 1 of 3, another computer) sits beside its title in dim.
- **Shizen** A second line appears on pointer or selection. Folds keep only their word.
- **Kokō** Colour only on what asks: a stopped container, an agent that needs you, a size past the guard's limit, your own pins.
- **Seijaku** Measured in the browser: rows are the blocks stacked in the panel, lines are text lines, glyphs are icons and marks, colours are chromatic colours. The phone screen and the picture are content and are not counted.
- **Seijaku** Rows fall less than half because most rows are the list itself: a port, a container, an agent. Those are the facts; none is dropped.
- **Shizen** States that need you are unchanged; the failure, empty and pending panel screens stay as drawn.

## No tool, no panel

### No tool guidelines

- **Seijaku** A panel that needs a tool exists only where the tool does: docker for Containers, xcrun simctl or the Android SDK's adb for Simulator, macOS or Windows for Memory. Without it there is no button, no Settings row and no job.
- **Seijaku** It replaces three sentences in empty panels: "Docker is not installed on this computer.", "Memory is not available on Linux." and Simulator's install rows.
- **Seijaku** The probe runs once when the window starts and again when Settings opens, so installing Docker later brings Containers back without a restart of kansō.
- **Seijaku** On a project that lives on another computer, Containers follows that computer's docker; Simulator and Memory stay on this one.
- **Yūgen** The Simulator's rule, hidden when no tool is found, becomes the rule for all three.

## Spend

### Spend guidelines

- **Kansō** At rest only what you would miss: header icons appear with the pointer in the panel or keyboard focus; the scope line goes, and a panel's one qualifier (another computer, the month, the device, 1 of 3) sits beside the title in dim; second lines and row actions appear on pointer or selection; folds keep their word. States that need you are unchanged.
- **Fukinsei** The month per plan comes first: one number and one sentence per subscription, for the calendar month, both at rest: 25× "Claude Max 20x: $5,012 of work for $200". Work is the month's sessions of that CLI at API list price; the plan is its monthly price. Under 1× the number and sentence turn working and say "not paying for itself", at rest.
- **Kansō** Detected, not typed. Each CLI's plan is read from one field, never a token: Claude Code ~/.claude.json → oauthAccount.organizationRateLimitTier (default_claude_max_20x → Max 20x) with billingType (stripe_subscription); no oauthAccount means an API key. Codex ~/.codex/auth.json → auth_mode only (chatgpt or apikey; the file's tokens are not read), and the plan from its newest session log, ~/.codex/sessions/YYYY/MM/DD/rollout-*.jsonl → payload.rate_limits.plan_type (plus, pro). Codex records the plan only once a session has run. Grok ~/.grok/settings_cache.json → payload.settings.subscription_tier_display (SuperGrok Plus); subscription_tier is empty, and Grok's price is not recorded. Pi records no plan: it runs on API keys.
- **Yūgen** Prices per plan come from a list shipped with kansō (new, plans.json): Claude Pro $20, Max 5x $100, Max 20x $200; ChatGPT Plus $20, Pro $200; SuperGrok $30, SuperGrok Heavy $300. Checked each release. A plan not in the list says so and takes your price in place.
- **Seijaku** No plan: API key use is plain cost, "paid as used", with no multiple.
- **Seijaku** Month just started: before the 7th, the number is the pace in dim, "too early to say".
- **Yūgen** Several computers: each computer's sessions are priced there and summed here, one line per computer; an unreachable one says so under it. The same Claude account on two computers (oauthAccount.accountUuid, an id) counts its plan once.
- **Shizen** Settings: sources are on when their folder holds sessions; a missing CLI says "not on this computer". Plans are detected rows, corrected in place (Enter keeps it; the correction is pending until saved). Add plan stays for what cannot be detected.
- **Kansō** One fact, one home: the multiple lives here only. The status bar's spend item stays the day's number.
- **Seijaku** States follow the state pattern: first read, empty, a read failed, a plan not read on its row.
- **Kokō** Cost: three small local files, read when the panel opens and once an hour after. Another computer: its plan and its month's rows come over the existing sync exchange, one call per synced computer per minute while the panel is open and in front, under 2 KB; nothing when closed.
- **Seijaku** New to build: the detector (three readers), plans.json, a Grok session reader (Spend reads Claude Code, Codex and Pi, core/config.mjs:10-35), the month-per-plan figure in costs.mjs, the plan row in the sync payload.
