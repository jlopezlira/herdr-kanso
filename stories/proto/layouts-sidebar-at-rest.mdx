## Sidebar at rest

### Sidebar guidelines

- **Shizen** In Dawn Mode, as in the other sidebar examples.
- **Shizen** The sidebar at full height. Every row the app has, in its place, on the sidebar grid: leads 15, names 8 after, a level every 23, rows 32 (50 with a second line), groups 12 apart.
- **Kansō** At rest a row is its lead, its name and its ensō. A second line only when needed (working, blocked), and the user chooses. + and ⋯ on the row under the pointer; no chevron, the row folds.
- **Kansō** A computer says its state in a word on its row (Off, Unreachable) with its one fix as a row button (power, refresh). Never a block under the row.
- **Yūgen** Asleep is one folded group with its count; a project in it wakes from its row.
- **Seijaku** Nothing moves: a row keeps its height and a name its width under the pointer, on focus and on a state change. Motion is the ensō spin and the working name's shimmer, nothing else.
- **Kokō** Colour only for a state, on the mark. Counts (↓12, 2) are muted.

## First read, empty, narrow

### Edge guidelines

- **Seijaku** First read is the kit skeleton: projects and their agents in the sidebar, a name, a name and its ensō, at 32, breathing. Rows replace it as they come; nothing jumps.
- **Yūgen** No project is the kit empty: one dim sentence and one action, New project. No sentence explaining what a project is.
- **Shizen** Narrow, nothing reflows: under 240 wide rows keep their first line only, the waiting one too (its wash and mark still say it); a branch after a name truncates first; the switch keeps its three words at 12.5. Under 160 it folds to the rail and opens again at the width you had.
- **Kokō** Cost: none of its own.
