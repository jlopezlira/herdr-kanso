## D1 One dialog skeleton

### Dialog skeleton guidelines

- **Seijaku** One shell for every dialog: popover, radius 8, padding 24 (K), 480 wide (popups 340). Title 15 semibold that names the thing (Rename checkout, not "Rename terminal"). An optional scope line, 11.5 dim, that is a fact. No paragraph, no label over a lone field.
- **Yūgen** Footer right-aligned: at most one solid primary (the kit's btn, 30, radius 6) and quiet links. Esc and a click outside close (assumed for Herdr dialogs; if a click outside does not close, Cancel comes back as a quiet link).
- **Kansō** The "Esc" printed in each corner goes: Esc always closes.
- **Seijaku** One spacing everywhere: 24 inside every dialog, window and the Settings dialog, on all four sides (K); panels keep 16; only the kit's gaps between parts. No dialog has a fixed height: it ends 24 under its last line, so no empty band at the bottom. Settings scrolls only past 680.
- **Kansō** A focused field wears the kit's focus ring, the only colour a dialog adds. Armed is the kit's: "Press again to …", accent, 4 s.
- **Shizen** First read: the kit skeleton, breathing. Pending: marked at once on its row (the kit's pending, S21: 80% and the breathing dot), nothing else changes until it is done. Failure: the source's own words in blocked (two lines at most, the full text in the tooltip), then one action row: the retry (Refresh for a read, the action's own name for a write), then Explain to \<agent\>. Empty: one dim sentence, one action at most. The same classes on every board (pax-fail, pax-empty, pax-skl, pax-ck pend).
- **Seijaku** Rename: "Empty: the name the program gives it" becomes the placeholder itself (claude): an emptied field shows what it falls back to.
- **Kansō** kanso-prompt-editor is removed. Nothing in kansō opens it, and it printed its title twice (as title and label). The Pin note is the same kanso-prompt with lines: 4.
- **Kokō** Remote: none. The answer is one hc label (on the terminal's computer) or one shared write.

## D15 Dialogs at rest, subtracted

### Dialogs at rest guidelines

- **Yūgen** Subtracted, measured on every dialog's first screen: rows 136 → 64, icons and marks 86 → 11, colours other than ink 10 → 4, pieces of text 245 → 106. No fact lost: the table says where each one appears when it matters.
- **Kansō** Rule: at rest a dialog shows what you came for and what you would miss; the rest appears on the row under the pointer or the keyboard, when it changed, or when it needs you. States that need you (failure, armed, blocked) stay as loud as on D1 to D14.
- **Kokō** Colour only on what asks: the solid primary, the accent of a focused field, an agent's ensō, the working colour on what will not come back. Everything else is ink or dim.
- **Kansō** kansō settings loses its header link to Herdr settings: the footer's Settings menu is the one door to both (D12). Keys folds its list into eight group lines under the keyboard. What's new drops the version line (the you-are-here node says it) and Refresh (it reads on open).
- **Kokō** Sleep a project says only what will not come back. Send lists only the agents that can take the text, with the project as a dim word on the row. Arrangements, Open with and Credits lose icons that did not tell rows apart.
- **Seijaku** These first screens are now the first screens on D2 to D10, D13 and D14, and those boards' states speak the same rows. The old first screens live only here, as before.
