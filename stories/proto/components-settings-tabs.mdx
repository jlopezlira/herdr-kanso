## D12 kansō settings and Herdr settings

### Kansō settings and Herdr settings guidelines

- **Seijaku** Two settings, named by owner everywhere: kansō settings (our dialog) and Herdr settings (the host app's window, app.settings). Neither is called just "Settings".
- **Kansō** One door, the ruling: the sidebar footer row is Settings with the gear exactly. A press opens one kit menu: kansō settings (⌃⇧,) and Herdr settings; when Herdr has an update waiting, a third row Update Herdr to 0.1.16, whose first press arms "Press again to restart Herdr" and whose second runs app.update. kansō's own update row in the footer stays as it is. In the rail the gear square opens the same menu. Herdr's logo is not used as an icon. ⌃⇧, still opens kansō settings directly.
- **Kansō** Tab bar: its gear and its ? go and the place closes up. ⌃⇧, still opens kansō settings on What shows, ⌘⇧/ on Keys.
- **Kansō** No link across: the footer's Settings menu is the one door to both. kansō settings' header carries no Herdr settings link (after the unified menu). Herdr's window is the host's and cannot link back either; the gear is always one click away.
- **Kansō** Nothing repeats. Theme, fonts, appearance, zoom, Option as Alt, sounds, usage data, ssh computers, and Herdr and herdr-core updates are Herdr's. In ours, Herdr's keys are one row that opens Herdr settings; Sounds is one row that opens Herdr settings · Alerts; the update switch is named Check for kansō updates.
- **Kansō** Removed: the account chip's click into app.settings, "Settings · update ready" with its dot, and the separate Update Herdr footer row (now the menu's third row).
- **Kokō** Remote: none. app.settings is local.

## D13 Settings, every tab, full screen (Stable Mode and Dawn Mode)

### Settings guidelines

- **Seijaku** Every tab of kansō settings as the whole dialog, 860 wide, dark and light, for audit, in Stable Mode and in Dawn Mode. The design and its states are on D2 (What shows), D3 (Keys), D4 (What's new), D5 (Credits) and D12 (how you get here).
- **Yūgen** Dawn Mode: the header carries board 11's band, rising on first load, on every tab. With Channel mark set to Pill, the Dawn Mode pill sits beside the title; set to Hidden, nothing.
- **Yūgen** The way in: the sidebar footer's Settings opens one menu, kansō settings or Herdr settings (D12). ⌃⇧, opens kansō settings on Pieces and ⌘⇧/ on Keys.
- **Seijaku** No area of What shows scrolls at 680; Keys and What's new scroll as documents.

## D2 Settings · What shows

### Settings guidelines

- **Yūgen** Asked first: what does a programmer come here to do? Bring back something they hid (often), hide what they are looking at (that happens in place: right-click, Hide this, board 10), and now and then look around. The page is built for that order.
- **Yūgen** The tab is What shows. It opens on Hidden by you: each hidden thing by name; the row under the pointer says where it lives and offers Show; Show all once. Nothing hidden: one dim sentence.
- **Yūgen** Everything else sits behind Browse the window (or Find): a small drawn map of the window: Sidebar, Tab bar, Pane header, Panels, Status bar, Notifications, plus Behind the scenes for work with no pixels. Pick an area and only its 3 to 11 rows show beside it. No area scrolls at 680.
- **Yūgen** One switch per row. "When needed" is no longer a choice: a piece that only speaks when it has something to say just does, and its row says so in three dim words (only when low, only after a turn). Three looks only where there are three: Channel mark (Artwork, Pill, Hidden).
- **Kansō** The ps / git / gh / lsof tags go from the rows. What hiding saves is said once, in the area's one line, only where it matters (Sidebar, Pane header, Status bar, Tab bar, Behind the scenes).
- **Kansō** Fewer rows, 52 ids kept in pieces.js: What each agent is doing writes sidebar.activity and sidebar.tasks (both read ps for the same rows; nobody hides one without the other); Computers writes sidebar.computers and footer.machine (the rows and the Add computer row go together); the guard's three switches show under Memory guard only when it is on.
- **Seijaku** Find searches every area; results name their area in dim; nothing found is one dim sentence.
- **Shizen** A switch acts at once, drawn at 80% with the breathing dot until shared state is written (S21) (the state pattern). Settings for this… from a right-click lands on the area with the row washed.
- **Yūgen** Presses. (a) Bring back a hidden piece: footer Settings, kansō settings, Show = 3 (⌃⇧, then Show = 2). (b) Hide what you are looking at: right-click, Hide this = 2; from here: 2 to open, the area, the switch = 4.
- **Kokō** Remote: none. Every switch is one shared write; hiding stops the job at once on every computer.

## D3 Settings · Keys, with Help's keyboard

### Settings guidelines

- **Kansō** Help's Keyboard and Settings' Keys were two homes for one fact: which key does what. They are now one page, Keys, in kansō settings. ⌘⇧/ opens it; the footer's Settings menu reaches it (the tab bar keeps no gear and no ?).
- **Kokō** Top: the keyboard. Holding a modifier, or clicking one, picks the layer. A kansō key is w12 with ink. A Herdr key is quieter. The picked key is solid. Accent is no longer used for "bound": accent means the window is asking.
- **Seijaku** Right of it: the picked key, what it does and its layer. Change starts capture on its row, so a key is rebound from the map or from the list.
- **Kansō** Below: one list of kansō's keys, grouped, with the picked key's row selected. Capture shows a breathing accent cap and "Esc keeps · ⌫ removes". A taken chord is the kit's Armed for 4 s. A changed row shows Reset. An unbound row shows a faint "No key".
- **Kansō** Families are one row each (⌃⇧1–9). The tab-bar tile strip that copied the tab bar goes.
- **Kansō** Group titles carry no count. Only Herdr's keys keep theirs (31), because their rows are not drawn here.
- **Seijaku** Pairs share a row (First and last, Slide left and right). The words match the commands: Refresh, not "Read again" or "Check now".
- **Seijaku** Herdr's own keys are one row that opens Herdr's settings, because kansō cannot rebind them.
- **Seijaku** Windows: a PC keyboard (Ctrl Win Alt), chords as Alt+L, the Alt layer first. Linux says Super.
- **Kansō** The two teaching lines ("kansō's own keys. Click one…", "Panes, tabs and spaces use Herdr's keys…") go.
- **Kokō** Remote: none. Writes keys.json on this computer. Herdr applies it as it lands.

## D4 Settings · What's new, a timeline

### Settings guidelines

- **Seijaku** The notes are a timeline: one line down the left, a node per release. Yours is filled and says you are here; releases above it are not installed yet; Dawn Mode builds have a node in dawn-sun.
- **Seijaku** Version 15 and the date beside the node, relative and absolute (today · Nov 11). The one action, Update to 3.31.0, sits on its node.
- **Kansō** Added, Changed, Fixed are quiet labels in a 64 column, said once per release beside the first of its lines, not headings that compete with the version.
- **Shizen** Older releases fold to one line (version, date, how many changes) and open in place.
- **Kansō** Updates (Stable Mode | Dawn Mode) and the version line with Refresh stay above it, as board 11. The automatic-check switch, the big Check button and the waiting banner stay removed.
- **Kansō** Dawn Mode: the kansō settings header carries board 11's dawn band, on every tab. The band arrives when the armed press is confirmed.
- **Kokō** Remote: one GET of CHANGELOG.md and one git ls-remote --tags when the page opens or on Refresh; nothing while it stays open.
