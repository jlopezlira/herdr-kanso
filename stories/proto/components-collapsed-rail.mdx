## S12 Collapsed rail

### Rail guidelines

- **Seijaku** Every element sits where the app draws it; the redesign goes on top. Width 76; the first 48 are the lights, with Dawn Mode's band behind them, 56 high, cut to the rail around its centre (11).
- **Kansō** Chrome in the kit's squares: show-sidebar first, centred on the line Herdr's logo held (its 32 square starts at 50), then + 8 below, both 32 with 17 (01). The view switch under them keeps its place and loses the blocked dot (S03). Herdr's logo is not an icon in the rail.
- **Seijaku** Tiles on the grid: the list starts 8 below the switch; a project is a 40 tile, radius 6, 4 apart, its initial 13.5 strong 2 above its count 11.5. A computer is its 15 icon; a remote one is dim while it can't be read, its projects folded under it until it is opened; this computer has no glyph, the list starts with its projects (S01). Before a remote computer a 24 hairline in border at 50%, centred, taking no height: decorative, so no contrast rule (A).
- **Kokō** Each project keeps the colour the app gives it (avatarColor from assignColors: ansi6, ansi3, ansi4, ansi5, ansi2, ansi1, least used first, kept in space.colors). In the kansō themes ansi1 to ansi5 are blocked, done, working, blue and violet; ansi6 has no token, so the board mixes blue and done half and half. A kept project with nothing open stays muted. The open sidebar has no initial (S04): the letter and its colour live here, where they are the only name.
- **Kokō** An unread or woken row is a foreground count: the initial already carries the project's colour and the accent ring belongs to the keyboard (K).
- **Kansō** Footer square: Settings only, the gear, opening the one settings menu (S19); Add computer is in the + menu. The block is its rows (56; 88 with an update row), as S19.
