## S18 Right-click menus

### Menu guidelines

- **Kokō** One menu, the kit's (10): 252 wide, rows 30, icons 14 muted, an 11.5 dim head naming what was clicked. Hints at the right in dim (when, where, last).
- **Kansō** Twelve rows, not twenty-four. Snooze, Move to project and Copy open their own short popup (›) instead of five presets, a project list and three copies inline. Sleep its project lives in the project's menu, its one home. Settle appears on Threads only.
- **Seijaku** Every menu ends the same way: the piece lines of the thing under the pointer, Hide “…” and Settings for this… (10).
- **Kansō** Armed in place. Close agent and Remove project become "Press again …" on accent for 4 s, the kit's Armed (K), and the menu stays open. Remove says how many tabs close.
