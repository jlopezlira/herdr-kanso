Contrast

Text is --fg, --muted or --dim. Each must keep at least 4.5:1 against every surface it can sit on (--canvas, --chrome, --bg, --raised, --popover) in both themes. The ratios below are measured from the published values with the WCAG 2 formula.

When to use

Kansō 簡素

  • Take every size, space, radius, height and colour from a token, in the app and in a design.
  • Pick the token by its role, not by the number that looks closest.
  • Write text in fg, muted or dim only, at 4.5:1 or more on every surface it can sit on.
  • Change tone on hover, focus and press; never the size or position of a box.

When not to

  • A raw number or hex colour in code, when a token holds that value.
  • A new token for one place. Use the nearest role, or ask for the set to change.
  • A palette value where the published value differs: the published one is the one that passes contrast.
  • Dawn colours on controls or text. They belong to the Dawn Mode art.

How to use it

  • kanso/tokens.json: every token: name, value and role, colour in dark and light
  • kanso/mods/kanso-panel/ui.js: the constants the app draws with: G, H, R, T, ICON, SWITCH, ARM_MS
  • kanso/themes/kanso.json: the dark palette
  • kanso/themes/kanso-day.json: the light palette

Give this to your coding agent:

Use only kansō's tokens. Read kanso/tokens.json for every size, space, radius, height, icon, switch, motion and colour (dark and light), and pick each by its role. In the app, draw with the constants in kanso/mods/kanso-panel/ui.js (G, H, R, T, ICON, SWITCH, ARM_MS). Write no raw number or hex colour that a token holds, and add no new token. Text is fg, muted or dim, and must keep 4.5:1 on canvas, chrome, bg, raised and popover in both themes. Hover, focus and press change tone only, never a box.
