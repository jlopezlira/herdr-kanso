What kansō does for you

Some actions end processes on your computer when you confirm them: putting agents to sleep, recycling or closing them from Memory, stopping a dev server from Ports, removing a worktree. kansō checks before each one and keeps what it can (an agent's conversation, a worktree's commits), but you decide when to run them and you are responsible for your machine and your work.
