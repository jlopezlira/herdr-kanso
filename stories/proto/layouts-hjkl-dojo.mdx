## hjkl dojo: playing

### Hjkl dojo guidelines

- **Yūgen** The example is the hero: 18 × 8 cells of 34, centred, as wide as the dialog allows. The cursor is the solid cell. The target is unmistakable: an accent ring, an accent wash and a dot. The best path appears only after a miss, as a trail with its first key on it ("l"), the hint the game already computes after 2 s (main.js:187).
- **Kansō** Round progress is the example's top edge: ten segments, the current one half lit. The 6 px bar and "2 / 10" under the example go.
- **Kansō** One quiet line above the example: score, time, streak, then the live count against the best, "2 moves · this one takes 5 at best". Over the best, the count turns working. The four stats in a grid go.
- **Kansō** Hachi speaks: the line is Hachi's, beside Hachi, in a bubble on raised. Hachi's face follows the mood (calm, glad, sweat, sleep; mascot.js:28-36, unchanged), and the bubble's edge takes the mood's colour (glad in done, sweat in working). The floating name and the grey box far away go. Hachi's art, its 16 × 15 grid and its fur and cream are the code's own.
- **Yūgen** The keys are the real keyboard under the example, four rows staggered as on a keyboard. The level's keys are raised; the rest are ghosts. The finger names appear once, under h j k l on the home row. The pressed key lights (solid); the hinted key takes the accent ring.
- **Seijaku** The levels are a path: a done level is a filled done node with its best; the current one is ringed and washed; one not tried yet is hollow. [ and ] move along it and p opens the pet chooser, outside a round. The game locks no level, and neither does the path.
- **Kansō** Keys only. A move starts a round, Enter plays again, n goes to the next level, Esc twice leaves. End round stays in the header for the mouse.

- **Seijaku** All nine done: the path is full, the total across it, and Hachi glad.
