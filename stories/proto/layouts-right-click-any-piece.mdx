## 10 Right-click any piece

### Right-click guidelines

- **Kansō** Every piece answers a right-click. Nothing is removed to keep the window quiet: a piece can be put away, and it comes back from the same place. Rest shows what you would miss. The rest stays one right-click away.
- **Seijaku** One menu, one order. The title names the piece (Pull request · #166, PR panel). Next come the piece's own actions, then a rule. Last come the same three lines on every piece: Only when needed, Hide this, Settings for this…. Rows that already have a menu (agent, project, tab) keep their actions on top and end with these three.
- **Shizen** Only when needed shows the piece only while it has something to say, such as a PR that is not ready or a check that failed. Hide this takes it off until you bring it back. Settings for this… opens Settings at that piece's switch, not at the top.
- **Kansō** One press decides. Hide this acts at once with no confirmation. A toast in the corner names what left ("Hidden: Pull request") and what stops with it (gh stops for headers). Its one action is Undo, for 8 s.
- **Fukinsei** Nothing moves around a hidden piece. Its neighbours close the gap on the same rhythm. They never jump or resize while the menu is open.
- **Kokō** The menu is the kit's menu: the same rows, height, hover and rule as every other context menu. A terminal's own right-click stays the terminal's.
- **Yūgen** Where to bring it back: right-click the region it lived in, or open Settings · Pieces, the one list of everything hidden.

## B10 Menus of the bars and headers

### Menus of the bars and headers guidelines

- **Seijaku** One menu for every right-click and popup in the bars: kansō's own, 252 wide, rows 30, radius 8 (K), icons 14 in muted, keys 11.5 in dim, the header 11.5 dim naming what was clicked. It replaces Herdr's menu for tabs (31, radius 7) and its new-menu.
- **Yūgen** Order everywhere: the thing's own actions, then the act that ends it, then the piece lines (Only when needed when the piece has it, Hide this, Settings for this…).
- **Kansō** An act that ends agents arms in place for 4 s and names what it ends: "Press again to close 2 agents".
- **Kansō** Pane menu: grouped by kind, with a header. Split left stays in the menu, with ⌥⌘⇧D shown. "Send right-clicks to pane" and "Use Herdr right-click menu" become one checked row, "Right-clicks go to the program". Find in conversation and Sleep join. Rows that cannot act are not drawn.
- **Seijaku** Separators come from groups, not from a row's index (menu/main.js:78).
- **Seijaku** The PR segment and panel button menus are board 10's, unchanged.
- **Seijaku** Keys: j k gg G Enter Esc, as in panels. Nothing is chosen until you move.
- **Kokō** Remote: 0. Pin state and agents are read from the window.
