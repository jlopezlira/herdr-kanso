## S08 While you were away

### Away card guidelines

- **Yūgen** A card, not a list header: raised on the sidebar's chrome, radius 8, a 1 px border, 4 inside. It is the one raised surface in the sidebar, so it reads as a note left for you; when there is nothing new it takes zero space.
- **Seijaku** It docks at the foot of the list, 12 above the footer, over empty space: no row moves when it comes or goes, and the list keeps its place.
- **Seijaku** On the 01 grid: the title row 32, "While you were away" 13.5 semibold with × (20 with 12) at its right. Each event a row of 32 (50 with a detail line): its icon 15 in its state colour, the fact 12.5 with the name medium, the detail 11.5 muted, the time 11.5 dim at the right. Four rows, then "+N more".
- **Kansō** It lists only what nothing in view says: a merge, an approval, a red check out of view, an agent that went to sleep or could not, an agent whose pane is gone. A PR verdict in a pane header in view, or an agent still on screen, is not repeated (L).
- **Shizen** A click opens the thing (the pane, the PR panel on the step, the Wake menu) and drops the row. × dismisses the batch. Right-click: Dismiss these, then the piece lines (10); Hide this switches it off for good, the same switch as its row in Settings · Pieces (sidebar.away: Show, When needed, Hide).
- **Kokō** Cost: none. Events come from what kansō already reads; they are written to the local database.
