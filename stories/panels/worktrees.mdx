### First screens guidelines

- **Kansō** Simplify information, never options. At rest a panel drops facts that repeat or say nothing now: scope lines, second lines, labels, marks and colour with no reason. Those facts come back under the pointer or keyboard. Every action and option, those the app has and those we added, stays visible and one press away. Colour stays only on what asks for you, and failures stay loud.
- **Seijaku** Measured in the browser on the six first screens: rows 103 → 76 · glyphs 35 → 19 · colours 33 → 25 · lines 150 → 88. Preview's 14 lines are the file itself, so its chrome is what halves: 2 rows to 1.
- **Kansō** One fact, one place. The folder, branch, files and lines are in the pane header, so the panels drop their scope and facts lines. Header icons, the period switch, Reply and Comment fields, Everyone's, Explain, Remove all and Prune all stay.
- **Kansō** Nothing is lost: each fact moved off the first screen says where it lives now.

### Worktrees guidelines

- **Seijaku** The kit header. + (a new worktree,), Share and Refresh are its icons; + also lives on the sidebar's project row. No scope on this computer; another computer names itself.
- **Kansō** The hero is one metric and one primary: Clean up 3 · 1.1 GB. The four stats under it go: they repeat the groups, the rows and the prune link. Explain to checkout stays beside it. Freed is one row at the end.
- **Kansō** No captions. What Clean up takes is said once, when it is armed, naming what goes. "Nothing uncommitted, every commit on a branch, idle a day" and "Clean up waits for the scan" go.
- **Yūgen** Groups are the repositories, open, with Show more, Remove all and Prune as quiet links; then Left behind.
- **Yūgen** An open repository's rows are one line, name and size. The verdict line shows only where it needs you (uncommitted changes, a broken link) or under the pointer. Picking a row opens its consequence, its path and its actions.
- **Kansō** A worktree open in a tab offers Go to cart-ui, never a sentence telling you to close its tabs.
- **Kansō** Armed Remove, Remove all and Clean up use accent, the kit's Armed, never blocked red.
- **Kansō** A group with two or more it can remove, once the scan is done, ends with Remove all 3 beside Show 2 more. Armed, the loss line sits above the links: what is lost in blocked, or "Nothing is lost." in muted, then "Merged branches go too." Rows open in a tab, locked, in use today or unchecked are never taken.
- **Seijaku** Over eight repositories, "Show 3 more repositories" follows the eighth group.
- **Kokō** Colour only where it needs you: uncommitted changes and a broken link. "In a tab" and "Merged" are dim words under the pointer, and named when Clean up is armed.
- **Kansō** "Ask your agent: Review the 2 that need a look" becomes Explain to checkout beside Clean up, and "Explain it" on a row becomes Explain to \<agent\>: the kit words. A worktree in a tab goes to that tab's agent; one in no tab goes to the agent of the pane in front.
- **Kansō** Freed is one row; its 30-day bars open from it. The second chart (disk each day) goes.

### Worktrees guidelines

- **Seijaku** The scan's progress takes the scope slot ("checking 12 of 40") instead of an 11 phase label squeezed beside the icons. The first scan uses the kit skeleton. Later scans keep the last result and draw Clean up disabled until the scan is done.
- **Yūgen** Empty: "No worktrees".
