## 01 Window at rest

### The real window, polished

- **Shizen** Drawn from the app as it is (capture 2026-11-12, kanso-spaces/main.js, kanso-chrome/main.js, kanso-tabs/main.js, kanso-strip/main.js, kanso-status/main.js). Same regions, same controls, same places. Polish only: nothing new, nothing removed.
- **Seijaku** One grid. A lead is 15 and its name follows 8 after, so each level steps 23: a machine's icon and a project's name at 8, its agents' icons under the machine's name at 31, a sub-agent's icon under its parent's name at 54. Rows are 32 (50 with a second line), groups 12 apart.
- **Seijaku** One size per role, from K. Chrome (+, sort, search, hide, footer) 32 with 17. Bars (tabs, panels, pane tools) 26 with 15. Row buttons (+, power) 20 with 12; row tails (chevron, branch) 12; marks 14. Names 13.5, tabs and headers 12.5, secondary 11.5. Controls radius 6, panes 8.
- **Kokō** Colour only for a state. ↓12 is a count, not a failure: muted, not red. Blocked lives on the mark (S21).
- **Shizen** The user chooses what a row says. Right-click an agent (board 10): What it is on, Time in its state and Commands still running each take Show, Only when needed or Hide. Drawn at Only when needed: Kylo-ren works and Fury waits, so they show a second line.
- **Kansō** Title bar: the lights only. Herdr's mark and name leave. The footer: Update kansō while one is ready, then Settings, whose menu holds kansō settings, Herdr settings and Help. The tab bar's own gear and help go there; the account chip goes (its agent count was a second home).
- **Fukinsei** The status bar is balanced: computer and branch at the left, the minimap in the middle, − 2 + at the right. Everything it shows stays.
- **Yūgen** No fold chevron: the row itself folds. Open and at rest, a row is its name: you can see what it holds. Folded, it shows what it hides in its place: the most urgent mark and how many. The header shows the worktree by its last name (app-audit), the full path on hover, so the branch is never cut.
- **Fukinsei** Nothing changes size. At rest a row is its lead, name and ensō. + and ⋯ appear on the row under the pointer in their own place; agile's + and Kylo-ren's ⋯ are not drawn at rest. Pane tools beyond sleep and send appear over the header's right end. A panel button shows its name only while its panel is open. No counting dots after a working name.
- **Yūgen** Selection is one wash: the active project, the active tab and the focused pane header share it. No coloured outline.
- **Kansō** Kept as it is: dark "Off" with its power button (you can act on it), the Update row, the tree lines, every panel button.
