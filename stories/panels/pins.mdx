### Pin guidelines

- **Seijaku** One pin, everywhere a picture is shown: Simulator (iOS and Android), Images, Preview pictures. Same marks, same note, same list, same Send.
- **Kansō** Entering: on a live picture (Simulator) the Pin tool in the header, or ⌥P, because a click there is a touch on the device. The picture freezes on the frame you saw (the screenshot simctl or the emulator already gives) and the scope line says "frozen at 14:06:12". A 2 px focus ring marks the mode; the device's buttons are not drawn while frozen. The tool again, or Esc, returns to live. Where a click means nothing else (Images, Preview pictures) the pin is always on and there is no tool.
- **Seijaku** Marks: a click drops a numbered pin; a drag draws a region, numbered at its corner. No strokes and no element picking: a device's picture has no DOM to pick from.
- **Kansō** The note is typed beside its mark, at once: click, type, Enter; the next click starts the next one. A click on a mark edits its note; Backspace on an empty note removes the mark. The dialog goes.
- **Yūgen** The list under the picture is the message, one line per mark, in the words that will be sent; × on hover removes one. One action: Send to checkout (the agent of the pane in front, named), ⌘↩. With none in front, Send to… opens the picker. It sends the frozen frame's path, the device and system, and the lines. After: "Sent to checkout 14:07", the marks clear, the picture is live again.
- **Kansō** Unsent marks are kept per agent and picture when you leave, and the scope line says "kept from 13:52 · not sent". A rotation or a device change since the freeze is said once; the marks stay on the frame you saw. A stalled picture freezes on its last frame and says so.
- **Kansō** An agent on devbox: the frame is copied there first, then the notes go with that path. Failures use the state pattern: the source's words, then Send.
- **Seijaku** Presses for one correction: the tool, a click, the words, ⌘↩. On Images and Preview: a click, the words, ⌘↩.
- **Seijaku** Reused: pinFrom for both marks; noted and aboutMessage, which gain a region form ("2 (region 3% to 97% across, 58% to 67% down)") and the device line; kept marks per agent and picture (, 47); deliver and sendKey (, 19); the waiting refusal (waitingNote,); Send. New: the inline note (replaces the kanso-prompt overlay,), drag regions, the frozen frame and ⌥P in kanso-sim, the copy to another computer, the shared classes.
- **Kokō** Cost: freezing reads no new file (the last frame is already on disk). Sending: one hc send; to another computer, one copy of the frame (about 300 KB) and one send.
- **Seijaku** Code annotates a live web page: picked elements, dragged regions, freehand strokes, one comment, a screenshot, then attach or send (contracts ipc.ts:816-948). kansō keeps its regions and its one-press send; it adds a note per mark, because a correction is usually several small things.
